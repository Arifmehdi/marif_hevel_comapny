About Hevel Capital.

A platform connecting growth-oriented operators with institutional capital -- built for long-term partnership.

Our Mission

Helping Operators Become
Investable.

Hevel Capital was created to help bridge the gap between growth-oriented convenience store operators and capital through a framework focused on transparency, reporting, governance, and long-term alignment.

While many operators have strong businesses and attractive growth opportunities, institutional capital often requires reporting standards, infrastructure, and ongoing oversight that smaller operations may not yet possess. Through the NLG Framework, Hevel Capital seeks to help operators build institutional-ready platforms while providing investors greater confidence, transparency, and accountability.

The NLG Framework's Objective Is Simple

We don't just help operators access capital. We help operators become investable.

Why Hevel Capital

Institutional Discipline. Convenience Store Specialization.

Hevel Capital was founded by Matt Kopsky, a commercial real estate and investment professional with experience spanning underwriting, credit analysis, asset management, investment research, and transaction execution.

Today, Matt brings that institutional experience to the convenience store industry, advising operators and investors on M&A transactions, sale-leasebacks, capital strategy, and growth.

Why Convenience Stores

Hevel Capital Focuses Exclusively On
The Convenience Store Industry.

The sector represents one of the largest and most fragmented retail categories in the United States, with approximately 150,000 locations nationwide and significant opportunities for consolidation, operational improvement, and institutionalization.

The combination of essential retail characteristics, recurring consumer demand, acquisition opportunities, and attractive real estate ownership structures creates a compelling environment for both operators and investors.
