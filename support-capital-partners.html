How We Support Capital Partners

Published: 2026 Edition • 12 Min Read

Greater Transparency Into Middle-Market Convenience Store Opportunities.

Hevel Capital helps capital partners access differentiated operator relationships supported by reporting discipline, governance practices, quarterly communication, and long-term alignment.

What Capital Partners Gain ?

Beyond capital, Hevel Capital provides the reporting, governance, strategic guidance, and long-term partnership operators need to build stronger, more institutional businesses.

01

Access To Vetted Operators

Rigorous operator selection and due diligence before any capital commitment.

02

Access To Off-Market Opportunities

Sector relationships and specialization surface differentiated deals.

03

Institutional Reporting Standards

Store-level visibility, KPI dashboards, and standardized financial reporting.

04

Quarterly investor reporting & communications

Business review packets and structured communication on a predictable cadence.

05

Governance Support

Meeting cadence, action tracking, and documentation that build confidence over time.

06

Long-Term Operator–Capital Alignment

Ongoing engagement, strategic advisory, and shared incentives across the hold.

Why The Framework Matters ?

Middle-market operators may have attractive businesses, but capital providers need visibility. The NLG Framework is designed to improve transparency, accountability, and confidence through reporting standards, governance practices, and ongoing engagement.

Who We Partner With?

Capital Partners Aligned For Long-Term Value

Hevel Capital works with capital partners who value transparency, disciplined reporting, governance, and long-term relationships with growth-oriented convenience store operators.

Partner Types:

Family Offices

Private investment groups seeking differentiated opportunities and long-term alignment.

Registered Investment Advisors (RIAs)

Advisors seeking structured opportunities supported by reporting and institutional discipline.

Private Investors

Investors looking for carefully evaluated convenience store opportunities.

What Happens After Closing ?

Hevel Capital remains involved after closing through investor communication, reporting oversight, quarterly reviews, strategic advisory support, and ongoing operator engagement.
